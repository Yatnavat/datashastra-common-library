Date Range Best Practices

  • Training period: Should contain sufficient historical data (recommended: 6+ months)
  • Testing period: Should be recent and representative of future conditions
  • No overlaps: Training and testing ranges should not overlap to prevent data leakage
  • Seasonal coverage: Include different seasons in training for better generalization
  • Chronological order: Training should typically come before testing in time
